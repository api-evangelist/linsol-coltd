# LINSOL Co.,Ltd.

> LINSOL (린솔) is a Daejeon, South Korea safety-technology company spun out as an in-house
> venture from Hanwha Aerospace. It applies acoustic detection techniques originally developed
> for defense landmine applications to real-time hazard, accident, and environmental detection.
> Its tagline is "Hearing the Invisible, Protecting the Invaluable." LINSOL ships hardware and
> integrated safety platforms rather than a public developer API — as of this writing there is
> no developer portal, API reference, SDK, or machine-readable specification published.
